Founded the game AI organization and competition ByteFight @ GT alongide <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/tylerkwok/"> Tyler Kwok</a> and <a href="https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~ladha/">Abrahim Ladha</a> based on my experience at MIT Battlecode. ByteFight has since been used in multiple classes as part of Georgia Tech's computer science curriculum to teach AI development to graduate and undergraduate students.

A team of developers now oversees the improvement of the platform, the annual ByteFight competition, and integrations with courses.
